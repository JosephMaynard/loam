# Acceptable use

LOAM is free, open-source software for people talking to each other: coordinating a gathering, staying
in touch during an outage, running a private conversation at an event, or keeping a community connected
when infrastructure isn't there. It is not intended to facilitate violence, abuse, harassment,
exploitation or fraud, and nothing about its design should be read as an invitation to use it that way.

Every member agrees to LOAM's rules before they post (the Welcome screen; the full text is at `/rules` on
any network). They prohibit harassment, threats, hate, sexual content involving anyone under 18,
intimate images shared without consent, posting someone's private details, scams, malware and spam. LOAM
is for adults (18+). Our child safety standards are at https://loamnet.com/child-safety.

LOAM has no central service, no user accounts, and no mechanism for the project's maintainers to see,
moderate, or shut down any individual deployment: each node runs entirely under the control of
whoever starts it. That means responsibility for a given deployment sits with its operator: the person
running a node decides who can join and what's enforced (moderation, message retention, the emergency
data wipe), and is responsible for that node's use complying with applicable law.

If you're considering a deployment involving sanctioned individuals or organizations, or in a
jurisdiction subject to specially designated trade or export controls, you should seek independent
legal advice before doing so. This document is not legal advice, and the maintainers have no way to
assess your specific situation.
